§ 01 — The problem

AI makes work fast.
Trusting it is slow.

01
No evidence trail
Agents hand back a result with no record of checks, sources used or changes made. Reviewers start from zero every time.
02
Chat is not a work record
Sources, requirements and follow-ups scatter across threads. Revisions rebuild context instead of building on a task.
03
Unbounded autonomy
Permissions, budgets and failed attempts are rarely scoped. "Done" means the model stopped, not that a person accepted it.
WHO FEELS IT  Small teams and professionals who delegate real deliverables to AI and remain accountable for the result.

§ 05 — Collaboration

Three parties,
one accepted result.

People share the workspace

Individual accounts, admin and member roles. Shared chat, tasks and sources. Only an explicit @bot invokes the assistant.

AI workers form teams

Roles and skills picked per task, dependencies scheduled by code. Autonomous claiming and SDK delegation are off by default.

Review, then accept

Machine checks, then an independent model. A person decides. Completion requires confirmation that execution stopped.

§ 06 — Core idea

Task Ontology: a shared model of work.

Objects, relationships, permitted actions and the evidence required for completion. Transitions are validated by program, not by prompt.

Scope: explicit schemas, stable references, validated transitions. General ontology reasoning and knowledge-graph inference are not claimed.

§ 10 — Trust by design

Autonomy inside three gates.

I

Scope & permissions

Tasks and authorization define scope. Roles and skills never expand permissions.

II

Lifecycle budgets

Limits span execution, verification, revision and failed attempts, so cost is judged across the whole delivery.

III

Check · review · accept

Machine checks and independent review precede acceptance. Interruption needs confirmed stop.

§ 12 — Innovation
Delivery, not just generation.
The unit of work is an accepted deliverable, modelled end to end by Task Ontology.
Teams governed by code.
Application code validates plans and writes control state. Models cannot grant themselves permissions or declare completion.
A reviewer that isn't the author.
A separate reviewer connection is mandatory for acceptance.
Files you own.
Markdown, YAML, JSONL hold the record. Indexes are disposable caches.
RESEARCH DIRECTION  A governed, self-organizing swarm. Dynamic team assembly exists today; self-organizing behaviour is exploratory.
